# Analogs

> Makers in the concept-as-play / explorable / interactive-learning space. Maintained by
> intelligence. Watch for inspiration and gaps to own, not for imitation.

## To study (seed list)
- Nicky Case (explorable explanations: games that teach systems). What works: playful,
  emotionally warm, shareable. 
- Brilliant (interactive learning at scale). What works: structure, retention. Gap: feeling.
- Veritasium / 3Blue1Brown (explainer craft on YouTube). What works: narrative + visuals.
- Indie edu-games and "serious games". 
- Bartosz Ciechanowski (deep interactive articles).

## For each, capture
What they make, what lands, distribution model, and the gap Makerchamber can own
(distillation + game-mechanic-as-concept + artsy indie feel).
