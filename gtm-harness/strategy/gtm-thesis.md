# GTM Thesis

> Why we go to market the way we do. Status: SEED, refine with Rahul.

## The bet
Makerchamber's quality bar takes time to reach, so we start building the audience and trust
now, in parallel with the games. We earn attention by teaching and showing the craft, not by
selling a product that is not ready. By the time the games are great, there is a warm
audience and an ecosystem that already knows us.

## How we earn attention (in order)
1. Teach and reflect in public (LinkedIn + YouTube devlogs). Trust compounds.
2. Show the work and the aesthetic (Instagram + Shorts). Wonder and discovery.
3. Be useful in the communities our people already inhabit. Relationships compound.
4. Build slow-burn partnerships with aligned ecosystem players.

## Why these channels
- LinkedIn: where the builder/founder/edtech-professional audience is, and where Rahul's
  personal credibility lives.
- YouTube: compounds and is searchable; native home for devlogs and concept-as-play; the
  indie-dev and explainer audiences live here.
- Instagram: visual reach and the artsy/indie brand surface.
- (No TikTok: India.)

## What success looks like (early)
Not revenue. Early signals: people playing the games and giving feedback, a growing
following that engages, inbound from aligned partners, and a clearer read on the real
problem from real learners.
