## What Makerchamber is
A browser game studio where the core mechanic of each game *is* the concept you are trying
to learn. Light, artsy, indie games. You do not read about a concept; you play it.

## The thesis (the why)
Complex ideas can be distilled to their first-principles components, then re-delivered in a
novel interactive setting where the player enacts those components as game mechanics. Done
well, this interactive digital medium is a powerful supplement to traditional education and
meaningfully lifts learner outcomes. Makerchamber is the proof.

## What we believe
- Understanding beats memorizing. A mechanic you played sticks; a paragraph you read fades.
- Distillation is the hard part and the moat: concept understanding + game design + product
  design + first-principles thinking, combined.
- Stylized and artful beats accurate-but-dry. Feeling matters in learning.

## Do / Don't
- DO teach, reflect, show the work, build trust before any ask.
- DO keep the artsy/indie texture in everything, including copy.
- DON'T use influencer engagement-bait, fake urgency, or growth-hack cadence.
- DON'T oversell or claim outcomes we have not seen yet. Honesty is part of the brand.
