# Outreach Templates

> Starting points only. Every real message must be personalized. Maintained by partnerships.

## Principles
- Reference their actual work in the first line.
- Lead with what we can offer them, not what we want.
- Propose one small, concrete thing. Keep it short.
- No mail-merge spray. If it could be sent to anyone, rewrite it.

## First-touch (creator / educator) — TEMPLATE
> Hi [name] — [specific genuine thing about their work]. I'm building Makerchamber, a studio
> making browser games where the game mechanic *is* the concept you're learning. [One concrete
> idea relevant to them.] No ask, just thought it might resonate. Happy to share a playable
> demo if useful.
