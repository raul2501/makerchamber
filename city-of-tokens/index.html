A Maker Chamber Game

City of Tokens

City of Tokens is a simple browser-based game whose mechanics are based on the transformer architecture, i.e the underlying logic of how Large-Language Models and modern-day AI work. I built this game in the process of studying and learning more about LLMs myself. A lot of this stuff can seem pretty magical and/or technically complex and intimidating (which is fair). This game will hopefully help you understand and appreciate it more intuitively.

There are multiple layers and processes taking place within an LLM from the time you enter a sentence into your favourite AI to the time it gives you a response. This game is divided into 8 levels, each of which will mimic a corresponding layer of the transformer architecture. At the end of this game, you should walk away with a better feel for what AI really is at a fundamental level.
