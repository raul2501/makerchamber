A Thought

What depth does

You just watched a word move through four floors and come out almost unrecognizable. The first floor gave it grammar. The second gave it a meaning. The third gave it a role. The fourth gave it a place in a real event. Each floor could only do its job because the floor before it had done its job first. That dependency is not a flaw — it is the point. Shallow patterns have to settle before deep ones can form.

By floor 12 (or 32, or 96), "bank" isn't really "bank" anymore. It's a high-level representation of a natural boundary feature in an erosion event — the specific role this token plays in this specific sentence.

What comes next
Now the last token in the sequence carries everything it has accumulated across every floor. That accumulated signal gets held up against every word in the vocabulary, one at a time, and scored: how well does each word fit what this representation is pointing toward? The scores become probabilities. One word is chosen. That is the prediction. That is Level 8: The Oracle.
