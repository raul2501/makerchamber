A Thought

What depth does

Each layer adds a layer of interpretation. Early floors handle surface features — grammar, word type, punctuation relationships. Middle floors handle semantics — word sense, entity type, domain. Late floors handle abstraction — role in context, reasoning, long-range dependencies.

By floor 12 (or 32, or 96), "bank" isn't really "bank" anymore. It's a high-level representation of a natural boundary feature in an erosion event — the specific role this token plays in this specific sentence.

What comes next
After all the floors, the last token in the sequence must speak. Its final representation — the sum of everything that happened across every layer — gets projected back onto the vocabulary. Every token gets a probability. One gets sampled. Level 8: The Oracle.
