The rule
You just placed three tokens — and the moment you did, the two sentences became different things. That distinction is why order has to be stamped in before the city processes anything. This is called positional encoding.

The paradox — now resolved
If attention processes everything simultaneously, how does it know word order?
It doesn't need to. Order was baked into each token in Step 2 — before the parallel step began. By the time attention runs, every token already carries its position as part of its identity.
Older systems had to read words one at a time to keep track of which came first. That was the only way to preserve order. The transformer skips that entirely — every token already carries its position before the parallel step begins, so nothing needs to wait. That front-loading is what makes processing everything at once possible.
The rule
Every token arrived at the parallel step already stamped with its sequence number. Nothing needed to wait for the token before it. That's why the whole sequence can be processed at once — and why the stamp has to happen first. This is called positional encoding.

What you discovered
The transformer reads everything at once — but only because position was stamped into each token first. Without positional encoding, "dog bites man" and "man bites dog" would be indistinguishable. With it, every token carries its sequence number as part of its identity, blended directly into its vector.

The same word at different positions creates different representations before any context-gathering happens. "only" at position 1 is already a different vector than "only" at position 3 — even before attention has run a single step.
Where things stand
Each token now has: a semantic position from the Map Room, a sequence position from the Census Bureau. They've been combined into a single starting vector. The token knows what it is and where it sits.

But it still doesn't know its context — it hasn't spoken to any of its neighbors yet. "bank" still has the same starting vector whether the next word is "river" or "deposit."
