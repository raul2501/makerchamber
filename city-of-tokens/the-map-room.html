Same word. Two completely different sentences.
"The bank was completely flooded."
"The bank was suddenly closed."
Two sentences, opposite meanings — the exact same point on the map.
Mapped.
What you just did is called embedding — every word becomes one fixed point on a map of meaning.

But “bank” proved one point can't hold two meanings. Fixing that is what the rest of the city is for.
