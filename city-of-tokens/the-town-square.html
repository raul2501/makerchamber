The mechanism

Three things into the square

Every token carries three vectors computed from its current representation.

Q
Query
What am I looking for? The question you bring. Your signal of need.
K
Key
What I advertise. My sign. What others see when they scan the square for help.
V
Value
What I actually hand over if chosen. The content that flows in when you listen.

The Key and the Value are not the same thing. A word can shout its presence and deliver little. A quiet word might carry everything you need. Your budget of attention is limited — choosing well is the skill.

The K ≠ V principle
Every token in the square broadcasts a signal and carries a separate payload. The broadcast is what others search for when scanning the crowd. The payload is what actually gets handed over if chosen. These two things are not the same — a word can be loudly present and deliver almost nothing, or keep quiet and carry exactly what you need. "the" and "after" are everywhere; their signals are strong. But when your attention landed on them, they gave you almost nothing back. The model learns this gap independently for each word. It is called the Key–Value separation.

What self-attention does

You just did this three times, in three forms. A token asked which neighbors were relevant, scored them, and built a weighted mix of what they offered. That mix became its new representation — carrying the context that resolved its meaning. Then the same ask ran twice in parallel, each pass tuned to different signals, and the results were combined.

The term for this is self-attention: every token attending to every other token, simultaneously, weighted by relevance. Running it in parallel across multiple learned perspectives is called multi-head attention. The scores come from Query × Key dot products; the payloads come from Values; softmax turns raw scores into weights that sum to 1.

What comes next
Attention changed "bank" — but it didn't erase what came before. Your original embedding, your position signal, and your new context all need to combine without any single update overwriting the rest. That's the job of the residual connection. Level 5: The Thread.
