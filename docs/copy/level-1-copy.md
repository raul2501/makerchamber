## Subtitle

City of Tokens is a simple browser-based game whose mechanics are based on the transformer architecture, i.e the underlying logic of how Large-Language Models and modern-day AI. I built this game in the process of studying and learning more about LLM's myself. A lot of this stuff can seem pretty magical and/or technically complex and intimidating (which is fair). This game will hopefully help you understand and appreciate it more intuitively. 

---

## Bridge paragraph

*(sits between subtitle and level grid)*

There are multiple layers and processes taking place within an LLM from the type you enter a sentence into your favourite AI to the time it gives you a response. This game is divided into 8 levels, each of which will mimic a corresponding layer of the transformer architecture. At the end of this game, you should walk away with a better feel for what AI really is at a fundamental level.
